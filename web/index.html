How long people stay

Of a hundred people hired, how many are still here after one, two and three years. People hired before the period count from the tenure they had when it began, so early leavers the file never saw are not credited as stayers.

What goes with resigning

An odds ratio of 2 means twice the odds of resigning for people with that sign, other signs held equal. It shows where to look, not what caused it.

Warning signs, team by team

People employed at the end of the period who carry two or more of the warning signs above. Counts per department only: this page never lists names, and teams under five people are not shown.

Use it for conversations, never for decisions about people
  • A team with many signs is a reason for a manager and HR to talk with that team.
  • Not for pay, counter-offers, promotion, training, ratings or dismissal: a decision taken on a predicted intention to leave is unequal treatment waiting to happen.
  • A scoring model built on this goes further, and is high-risk under the AI Act: see the model card.

The gender pay gap

Base pay of people employed at the end of the period, men as the reference, as Directive (EU) 2023/970 measures it.

Base pay only. A Directive report also counts bonuses, allowances and benefits, and the gap by category of workers: the pay transparency kit does that part.
